The register for yard sales where everyone gets their fair share.

Johnny's tools, Mary's dishes and Paul's old bike, all on one table. Ring up each sale on your phone and SplitTheSale keeps track of whose stuff sold, splits "$30 for the lot" deals fairly, and tells everyone who pays whom when the day is done.

How it works

  1. Set up your sale

    Add the families selling, and say where money goes: the cash box, or straight into someone's Venmo.

  2. Ring it up

    Tap whose item it is and type the price. Got an offer for the whole pile? Tap Set Total and the discount is shared out fairly.

  3. Settle up

    At the end, SplitTheSale shows exactly who pays whom, so nobody's doing math on the back of a receipt.

Your data, then gone

We keep an event's sales only while it's running and for 6 months after it closes. When it closes we email you a full report and spreadsheets to keep, and after 6 months the event and every sale in it are permanently deleted. Free sales are deleted at midnight. Card payments for events are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card number.
